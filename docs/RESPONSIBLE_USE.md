# Responsible use

This project studies integrity signals in unauthenticated, safety-critical
telemetry. It may help a feeder operator notice inconsistent observations or a
degraded local pipeline. It cannot authenticate an aircraft, verify its position,
attribute intent, establish that spoofing occurred, or support aircraft separation.

Use only receivers, data, and systems you own or are authorized to test. Follow
local radio, privacy, aviation, and data-license rules. Do not use the software to
track individuals, target aircraft, interfere with transmissions, automate
enforcement, or publish sensitive receiver/aircraft histories. Keep a human in the
loop and corroborate any operational concern through authorized independent
sources.

Public claims must preserve the system's vocabulary: `NOMINAL`, `QUESTIONABLE`,
and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` describe evidence quality. `QUESTIONABLE` does not mean
malicious, and source unavailability is not an aircraft anomaly.
